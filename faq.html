FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most.

Do I need experience to join a trip?

No. We run trips for every level, from complete beginners to advanced surfers. Each tour page shows its recommended level.

What equipment is included?

Most tours include a board and wetsuit. Check the 'What's included' list on each tour page for details.

How do I book?

Open any tour, fill in the booking form and our team will confirm availability within 24 hours.

What is your cancellation policy?

You can cancel free of charge up to 14 days before your start date. After that, a partial charge may apply.

Is it safe?

Safety is our priority. Instructors are certified, groups are small and we brief you on conditions before every session.

Can you arrange flights and accommodation?

Surf & Stay tours include accommodation. Our concierge can also help with flights and transfers on request.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, reef-safe sunscreen, a towel and a water bottle. We will send a full packing list after you book.
