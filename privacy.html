Privacy Policy

How we look after your information.

Last updated: November 2026

Information we collect

We collect the details you give us when you book a tour, create an enquiry or contact us, such as your name, email address and preferences.

How we use it

  • To process bookings and answer your questions
  • To send you information about your trip
  • To improve our website and services

Payments

Payments are encrypted and transmitted securely using SSL. We do not store full card details.

Sharing

We only share information with guides, hosts and payment providers needed to deliver your trip. We never sell your data.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing contecttraval@gmail.com.
