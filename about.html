About us

Hand-picked guides, genuine experiences and a passion for the ocean.

Surf trips built by surfers

We started as a small group of friends who wanted to share the best waves we had found. Today we work with hand-picked guides, coaches and local hosts in more than a dozen countries.


Whether you are standing on a board for the first time or chasing bigger swells, we plan your trip around your interests, level and budget, and look after the details so you can focus on the ocean.
